Never Have I Ever – Present Perfect

Players

-

How to play

  1. A sentence appears: Never have I ever ___ …
  2. The infinitive is hidden. The teacher may reveal it if needed.
  3. The timer starts only when the teacher clicks Start timer.
  4. When the timer ends, the correct answer is shown.
  5. The teacher decides if the answer is correct (✅) or wrong (❌).
  6. Players who have done it raise their hand and are selected.
  7. One storyteller is chosen and evaluated.

The first player to reach 5 lives, wins!
